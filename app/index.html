📧 Weekly Digest Email

When enabled, sends automatically every Monday 8am (ET) — upcoming birthdays/anniversaries, upcoming events, and anything queued from the Events tab's "Add to This Week's Family Email."

🎂 Family Calendar — bulk import

Paste lines in any of these formats, one per line:

09/18/86 Molly Ryan Martin — birthday
09/10/94 Jack & Jeanette Anniv — anniversary
10/17/14 Death Isabel Rohrbaugh — death anniversary
10/28/38 – 01/14/26 Pat Gibble — birthday, passing noted

📇 Address Book — bulk import

Paste family blocks separated by a blank line. First line = household name, second line = address, remaining lines = one person per line as Name | phone | email (phone/email optional):

Jack & Jeanette Martin
1132 Bridgewater Dr, New Oxford PA 17350
Jack Martin | 717-577-5853 | j-jmartin@comcast.net
Jeanette Martin | 717-225-1827 | jeanettewm1954@gmail.com

If a person's email matches an existing approved account, that account gets linked to the household instead of creating a duplicate no-account contact.

🔗 Link Calendar Birthdays to Contacts

Matches calendar birthdays to contacts by exact name and lets you confirm/fix the guessed year before applying (2-digit years are ambiguous — the same digits can mean 1917 or 2017 depending on the person, so nothing is applied without your review). Once applied, the calendar entry and the contact stay linked — re-running this won't re-suggest or duplicate it.

No matching contact — add to a household
Common when a birthday belongs to a kid who was never in the address book, or the name is spelled slightly differently between the two lists.
